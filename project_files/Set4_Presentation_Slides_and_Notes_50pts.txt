SLIDE 5: WORKFLOW 2 — WATER QUALITY & H2S SAFETY (FOR TEAMS > 4)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Bullets:
• Solves deadly confined-space hazards (Hydrogen Sulfide gas pockets, low oxygen, illegal acid/chemical dumping).
• Ingests portable multi-gas & water probe readings (`H2S ppm`, `O2 %`, `pH`, `Turbidity NTU`) before manhole entry.
• Safety Decision Gate: If `H2S >= 10 ppm` or `O2 < 19.5%`, automatically locks the work order, mandates forced ventilation + SCBA gear, and alerts the Safety Officer.

Before workers enter any underground culvert, sensor telemetry is checked against OSHA limits and AI anomaly detection—locking entry within seconds if toxic H2S gas is present."
