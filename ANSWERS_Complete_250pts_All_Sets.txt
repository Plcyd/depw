[WORKFLOW 1 SPECIFICATION — BARANGAY DECLOGGING & GIS SMART TRACKER]:
1. Trigger Event: HTTP Webhook / Web Portal submission containing Citizen Name, Contact Number, Explicit Consent Boolean, Barangay No., Street Address, GPS Latitude/Longitude, Text Description, and Site Photo URL.
2. Node 1 — Consent & Privacy Sanitizer (Rule-Based Script):
   - Verifies `consentAccepted === true`.
   - Applies regex filters to description text to strip 12-digit IDs, credit cards, or passwords before logging.
3. Node 2 — Spatial Deduplication & GIS District Assignment (GeoJSON Engine):
   - Calculates Haversine distance against active tickets from the last 48 hours. If distance <= 100m, increments `upvoteCount` on existing Ticket ID and sends duplicate-merge receipt.
   - Runs point-in-polygon check against Manila Districts I–VI GeoJSON boundaries to auto-populate `assignedDistrict`.
4. Node 3 — AI Multimodal Classification & Triage (Gemini 2.5 Flash API):
   - System Prompt: "Act as a Municipal Drainage Triage Engineer. Analyze the citizen report and image. Return strict JSON with keys: `obstructionCategory`, `severityScore` (0-100), `priorityLevel` (Critical/High/Medium/Low), `recommendedEquipment`, `estimatedCrewSize`, and `triageSummary`."
5. Node 4 — Conditional Dispatch Router:
   - IF `severityScore >= 80` OR near hospital/school: Trigger instant Priority Webhook to District Duty Engineer + reserve Vacuum Jetter Unit.
   - ELSE: Assign to next scheduled barangay declogging cluster route.
6. Node 5 — Database Persistence & Citizen Notification:
   - Writes structured record to Central Dispatch Tracker and dispatches SMS/Web confirmation with Ticket Reference ID.

[WORKFLOW 2 SPECIFICATION — DRAINAGE WATER QUALITY & H2S SAFETY TELEMETRY]:
1. Trigger Event: Field crew submits pre-entry multi-gas & water quality probe telemetry via mobile form or IoT webhook.
2. Node 1 — Threshold Rule Engine: Evaluates OSHA/NIOSH confined-space limits (`H2S < 10 ppm`, `O2 >= 19.5%`, `6.0 <= pH <= 8.5`).
3. Node 2 — AI Environmental Anomaly Analyzer: Evaluates multi-parameter trends (e.g., sudden pH drop + high turbidity indicating illegal chemical dumping upstream) and generates specific PPE & ventilation instructions.
4. Node 3 — Automated Permit Gatekeeper: Issues digital entry clearance or locks ticket status to `HOLD_HAZARD_VENTILATION_REQUIRED` and alerts the Safety Officer.

SET 3 — QUESTION 1 (35 Points)
Prompt: How does your workflow use AI to improve the process? (Explain what makes it smarter than a simple rule-based automation.) *
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[COMPLETE ANSWER]:
A simple rule-based automation (such as basic IF/THEN keyword filters or standard form-to-sheet scripts) fails in real-world municipal reporting because citizens write in unstructured English, Tagalog, or Taglish, use inconsistent street descriptions, and upload photos without technical labels. For example, a rule looking for the word "flood" might miss a critical report that says "Tubig hanggang tuhod sa tapat ng health center, barado ng sako-sakong basura ang imburnal" ("Knee-deep water in front of the health center, drainage blocked by sacks of garbage") or over-prioritize a minor puddle just because the user typed "URGENT!!!" in all caps.

Our workflow uses Multimodal AI (Gemini 2.5 Flash) to make the system genuinely intelligent in four ways:
1. Visual Severity & Obstruction Recognition: Instead of relying on user checkboxes, the AI inspects the uploaded site photo to distinguish between surface litter (solvable by a 2-person manual crew with declogging rods) and compacted silt, grease buildup, or a collapsed concrete box culvert (which requires dispatching a heavy Vacuum Jetter truck or Clamshell excavator).
2. Multilingual & Contextual Understanding: The AI naturally understands English, Tagalog, and Taglish descriptions, extracting the exact landmark, water depth, and public risk (such as proximity to schools, hospitals, or main arteries) even from informal messages.
3. Multi-Factor Dynamic Priority Scoring (0–100): Rather than a rigid FIFO queue, the AI synthesizes visual evidence, text context, weather conditions, and GIS flood-zone elevation to generate an objective 0–100 Urgency Score and a 1-sentence engineering briefing for the crew.
4. Intelligent Hazard & Anomaly Synthesis (Workflow 2): In our companion Water Quality & H2S Safety workflow, AI correlates subtle multi-sensor patterns (such as gradual H2S rise combined with abnormal pH and turbidity) to identify upstream illegal chemical dumping vs. normal organic sewer gas—predicting toxic spikes before they breach static alarm thresholds.
